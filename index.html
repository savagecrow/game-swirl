How to Play

Swirls of the same color that touch (up / down / left / right) form a combo. Click a swirl to highlight its group and see its point value, then click again to remove it. Swirls fall, and empty columns slide left. Click elsewhere to cancel. Keyboard: arrows move, Enter/Space selects/removes, X shows goals.

Scoring: a combo of n swirls = 4n² − 4n − 4 (2→4, 3→20, 10→356, 40→6236). A lone Single costs 100. Emptying several columns in one move gives a column bonus (25, 75, 150, 250, 375 …). Clear the whole board with no Singles and the score is doubled.

Level Challenge: beat five goals each level - Largest Combo, max Singles, Big Column Bonus, Total Combos, Score. Optional 120s timer: each second left is worth 50.

Single Game: just maximise your score. Versus: take turns on one board (2-4 players, human or CPU). Your color's combos score double; your own Singles cost 200; lose your last swirl of your color and you're out. Split Screen: real-time race on separate boards; clear a color and it vanishes from every board. First to the goal score wins.
